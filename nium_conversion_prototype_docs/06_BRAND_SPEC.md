# 06 — Brand and Visual Specification

## Direction
Restrained institutional fintech for business payments and treasury. Trustworthy, precise and operationally clear—not consumer crypto, gaming or speculative trading.

## Principles
Clarity before decoration; clear amount hierarchy; neutral surfaces; colour communicates state; asset/network always visible; dense data remains readable. Avoid neon, gradients, coin illustrations and trading-terminal styling.

## Suggested tokens (prototype only; not official Nium brand)
Navy `#102A43`; deep navy `#0B1F33`; blue `#2563EB`; teal `#0F766E`; background `#F5F7FA`; surface `#FFFFFF`; text `#172B4D`; secondary `#627D98`; border `#D9E2EC`; success `#16803C`; warning `#B7791F`; error `#C53030`.

## Typography
Inter/system sans; tabular numerals for money; clear numeric alignment; no decorative display fonts.

## Components
Balance cards, account selectors, amount input, quote summary, expiry indicator, primary/secondary buttons, text-labelled status badges, transaction table and inline errors.

## Brand asset
Do not invent/reproduce official Nium logo. Without approved asset, use `Nium Wallet Prototype` text wordmark and label as interview prototype, not official production UI.
